## Strengths

- The Drive Line sequence is intact, avoids a generic catalogue, and uses dark/light pacing to move from desire to action.
- The hero makes the vehicle and primary discovery CTA obvious; its copy is concise and `Xe có sẵn` is subordinate.
- `Bản minh hoạ` avoids fabricated commercial facts; `next/image`, focus styling, labelled navigation, one H1, and reduced-motion handling are sound foundations.

## Highest-impact findings

### P0 — Must fix

1. **Conversion actions are not real destinations.** Important CTAs point to generic in-page anchors, sometimes unrelated to their label (for example, configuration, inventory detail, advisor contact). This breaks the core promise that the next step is obvious.
2. **Finder and comparison do not complete a decision.** Finder has no Back, visible answer history, rationale, or model-specific continuation. Compare only gathers names; empty slots are inert and the three-item limit fails silently.

### P1 — High impact

1. **Mobile CTA is permanently fixed.** It appears before context is earned and can conflict with the final CTA or controls. It should activate only after model/finder context and change its label with that context.
2. **Confidence sections state reassurance rather than prove it.** Inventory, ownership, and proof are visibly labelled demo, but lack a useful process explanation, supported product status, source/evidence pattern, or actual advisor handoff.
3. **Product identity is weak in model lanes.** Hue-shifted or reused imagery cannot reliably communicate 911/Taycan/Macan/etc.; current alt text refers to implementation language such as “option 3”.
4. **Touch and state feedback are insufficient.** Family/text controls are materially smaller than 24px on mobile; account/save semantics conflict; max compare, loading, empty, and error states are absent.

### P2 — Polish / later commands

- Move static sections out of the page-wide client component; use `next/font` rather than CSS `@import`; replace CSS background PNGs with responsive image components.
- Increase status-label contrast, add a skip link, and use product/scene-specific alternative text.
- Tighten typography toward engineered product UI: the large Prata moments are elegant but read editorial-luxury more than precision automotive.

## MDOP mandatory summary

- **Biggest conversion blocker:** labels promise concrete commerce routes but deliver generic anchors.
- **Biggest visual weakness:** repeated/hue-treated mock crops make product identity feel generic.
- **Biggest mobile weakness:** the always-on bottom CTA is detached from user context and can obstruct completion.
- **Biggest hierarchy problem:** Configure, Compare, and Inventory labels lack a committed next state, so visual hierarchy does not map to action hierarchy.
- **Biggest trust/friction issue:** the finder and inventory surfaces disclose that they are demo but do not show what happens after the decision.
- **Single highest-impact improvement:** make one model-selection path genuinely continuous: selected family → explained finder result → model detail/configuration → saved/consultation context.
